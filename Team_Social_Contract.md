## Section 1: Conflict Resolution Protocol

When a "Site War" or inter-team conflict arises, we follow this 3-step process:

### Step 1 — Name the Issue, Not the Person
State what the technical or process disagreement is without blaming any individual or site.

Example:
"There is a conflict about the API response format"

NOT:
"The Berlin team wrote a bad API"

### Step 2 — Neutral Ground Discussion
A dedicated 30-minute meeting will be scheduled to discuss the conflict.
Both sides explain their perspective.
The PM or Coordinator facilitates the discussion without taking sides.

### Step 3 — SOP Creation
Every resolved conflict produces a documented Standard Operating Procedure (SOP).
The resolution is added to Decisions_Log.md.

### Escalation Path
If the conflict is not resolved within 3 days, it escalates to the London PMO for final arbitration.

## Section 2: Communication Norms

### Golden Window Hours
All 4 sites must be available simultaneously during:

Monday–Thursday | 10:00–12:00 GMT

(Cairo: 12:00–14:00 EET | Berlin: 11:00–13:00 CET | Bangalore: 15:30–17:30 IST | London: 10:00–12:00 GMT)

### 24-Hour Response Rule
Any Slack message or Jira comment from another site must receive an acknowledgment within 24 hours.

Example:
"Seen — will respond by [date]"

### Meeting Protocol
- All meetings must have an agenda shared 24 hours before the meeting.
- Maximum meeting size is 6 people.
- Decisions must be documented within 2 hours after meetings.

### Escalation for Blocked Items
If any developer is blocked for more than 4 hours, they must create a blocker flag in Jira and tag the PM directly.
Silent blocking is considered a Social Contract violation.

## Section 3: Ethical Commitment

### Handling "Ignore a Bug" Requests

Per the ACM/IEEE PUBLIC Principle:

"Software engineers shall act consistently with the public interest. Bugs affecting patient health or data security are NEVER acceptable to suppress."

### Our Commitment

1. Any known bug affecting patient data or security MUST be reported.
2. Sensitive issues will be tracked using a private Jira issue type called CONFIDENTIAL-BUG.
3. No team member will face retaliation for reporting legitimate technical concerns.
4. The PM is ethically obligated to report all known issues to the GlobalCare Board.

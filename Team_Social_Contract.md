## Section 1: Conflict Resolution Protocol
When a "Site War" or inter-team conflict arises, we follow this 3-step process:

**Step 1 Name the Issue, Not the Person:**
State what the technical or process disagreement is, without attributing blame to an individual or site.
*Example: "There is a conflict about the API response format" NOT: "The Berlin team wrote a bad API"*

**Step 2 Neutral Ground Discussion:**
Schedule a dedicated 30-minute call to address the conflict. Both parties present their perspective. The PM or Coordinator facilitates—they do not judge.

**Step 3 SOP Creation:**
Every resolved conflict produces a documented Standard Operating Procedure (SOP). This becomes the binding norm and is logged in Decisions_Log.md.

**Escalation Path:** If the conflict is not resolved within 3 days, it escalates to the London PMO for final arbitration.

## Section 2: Communication Norms
**Golden Window Hours:**
All sites must be available simultaneously during: Monday-Thursday | 10:00-12:00 GMT
*(Cairo: 12:00-14:00 EET | Berlin: 11:00-13:00 CET | Bangalore: 15:30-17:30 IST | London: 10:00-12:00 GMT)*

**24-Hour Response Rule:**
Any Slack message or Jira comment from another site must receive at least an acknowledgment within 24 hours. "Seen, will respond by [date]" is an acceptable acknowledgment.

**Meeting Protocol:**
- All meetings have an agenda shared 24 hours in advance.
- Maximum meeting size: 6 people.
- Decisions are always documented in the meeting notes within 2 hours.

**Escalation for Blocked Items:**
If any developer is blocked for more than 4 hours, they must post a blocker flag in Jira and tag the PM directly. Silent blocking is a Social Contract violation.

## Section 3: Ethical Commitment
**Handling "Ignore a Bug" Requests:**
Per the ACM/IEEE PUBLIC Principle: "Software engineers shall act consistently with the public interest. Bugs affecting patient health or data security are NEVER acceptable to suppress."

**Our Commitment:**
1. Any known bug, especially one affecting patient data, MUST be reported regardless of who asks you to stay quiet.
2. We maintain a private Jira issue type "CONFIDENTIAL-BUG" for sensitive disclosures.
3. No team member will face retaliation for reporting a legitimate technical concern (Safe Harbor).
4. The PM is ethically obligated to report all known issues to the GlobalCare Board.
